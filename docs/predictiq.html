01 / Problem & data

Predict total hourly rentals (cnt) for historical scenarios. The UCI Bike Sharing dataset covers Washington DC in 2011–2012. The chronological split contains 12,139 training, 2,640 validation and 2,600 held-out test observations.

To avoid target leakage, casual and registered rental counts are excluded because their sum is the target. The instant identifier is also excluded. Preprocessing is fitted only on training data, including within each cross-validation fold.

03 / Engineering decisions

Three expanding chronological cross-validation folds compared four Random Forest configurations. The selected configuration uses 70 trees, maximum depth 18 and minimum leaf size 2; its training-only CV MAE was 54.4441.

Two engineered features capture commuting-hour patterns and temperature–humidity interaction. The training pipeline is saved locally for consistent inference; the earlier model is preserved for comparison.

06 / Limitations & next steps

The held-out 2,600 observations remain unused. One validation example on 1 August 2012 at 18:00 had 891 actual rentals versus 409.92 predicted, an absolute error of 481.08. Its cause is not established. Historical data cannot establish present-day forecasting performance.

The raw dataset and trained joblib artifact are excluded from GitHub. New installations must reproduce training; never load untrusted joblib or pickle files. Public hosting and final held-out evaluation remain future work.
