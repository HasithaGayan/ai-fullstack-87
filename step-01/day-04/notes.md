## Semantic HTML

Semantic HTML means using HTML elements according to their meaning.

- header = top/introduction area
- nav = navigation links
- main = main page content
- section = related group of content
- article = self-contained content
- footer = bottom/page information

## Heading Structure

- h1 = main page heading
- h2 = major section headings
- h3 = headings inside sections or articles

## Internal Links

href="#projects" connects to id="projects".

## Important Semantic Decisions

- nav for navigation
- ul and li for lists
- section for major portfolio sections
- article for project and education content
- clear link text instead of "click here"
